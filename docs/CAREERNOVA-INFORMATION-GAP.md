# CareerNova Information Gap Analysis
**System:** CareerNova (SIH25094)  
**Date:** 2026-08-16  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Official government portals in Jammu & Kashmir (such as J&K Samarth, JKBOSE, JKBOPEE, and PMSSS) provide robust transactional functionality: processing applications, conducting examinations, and publishing seat allotments. However, these systems assume that the student **already knows** what decision to make, which degree to choose, and what eligibility rules apply.

CareerNova bridges this **Cognitive & Discovery Information Gap**.

---

## 2. Official Portal vs. Student Gap Matrix

| Official Portal | What the Official Portal Solves | What the Student Still Has to Figure Out | How CareerNova Solves the Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **J&K Samarth Admission Portal** (`jkadmissions.samarth.ac.in`) | • Seat allocation engine<br>• Preference list submission<br>• College application filing | • Which degree major fits their stream qualification.<br>• Which courses align with their interests.<br>• What alternatives exist if cutoffs are high.<br>• What postgraduate pathways a degree opens. | Provides stream-qualified course exploration, non-predictive directional alignment, and institutional availability **before** Samarth preference locking. |
| **J&K Board of School Education (JKBOSE)** (`jkbose.jk.gov.in`) | • Syllabi & scheme of studies<br>• Board exam timetables<br>• Official marksheet issuance | • What stream (Science/Commerce/Arts) to choose after Class 10.<br>• What subjects in Class 11 open specific college options.<br>• What questions to consider before locking streams. | Provides Class 10 stream discovery, subject breakdowns, and key decision considerations without premature UG/college predictions. |
| **JKBOPEE Portal** (`jkbopee.gov.in`) | • Professional entrance registration<br>• Merit rank list publication<br>• State engineering/medical counselling | • Whether professional healthcare/engineering is realistic vs. general B.Sc/B.Tech.<br>• What non-engineering technical degrees exist (e.g. BCA/B.Sc CS). | Contextually introduces JKBOPEE professional pathways only when students explore health/engineering signals. |
| **AICTE PMSSS J&K Portal** (`aicte-jk-scholarship-gov.in`) | • Scholarship registration<br>• Document verification workflow<br>• Scholarship fund disbursement | • Whether their chosen degree qualifies for PMSSS.<br>• What academic eligibility caps apply.<br>• Application timeline awareness. | Integrates PMSSS eligibility criteria and direct portal links alongside relevant Class 12 degree options. |
| **University of Jammu & University of Kashmir** (`jammuuniversity.ac.in` / `kashmiruniversity.net`) | • Statutory academic ordinances<br>• Affiliation rules<br>• Examination calendars | • How to interpret statutory minimum eligibility strings.<br>• What higher study options exist after graduation (e.g. CUET-PG). | Translates statutory eligibility into clear, student-friendly minimum application requirements and links higher study pathways. |
| **JKPSC & JKSSB Portals** (`jkpsc.nic.in` / `jkssb.nic.in`) | • Recruitment notifications<br>• Online job applications<br>• Exam syllabus downloads | • Which undergraduate degrees satisfy educational requirements for public service exams.<br>• Long-term career pathway awareness. | Maps government recruitment exam avenues to relevant degree courses as long-term context (avoiding deterministic guarantees). |

## 3. The Core Product Proposition

```
[ Fragmented Government Portals ] ──► [ Student Confusion & Misalignment ]
                                              │
                                              ▼
                                 [ CAREERNOVA DECISION LAYER ]
                                              │
                                              ▼
[ Stream Qualification + Signal Exploration ] ──► [ Informed Next Decision on Official Portal ]
```

CareerNova does **not** compete with or replicate government portals. It functions as the **pre-admission guidance layer** that enables students to make informed, confident choices on official platforms.
