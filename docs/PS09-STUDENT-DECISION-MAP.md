## PART A — THE ACTUAL J&K STUDENT PROBLEM

### Surface Problem (what students say)
"I don't know what to study after Class 10 / Class 12."

### Deeper Problem (what is actually happening)
A student in J&K faces compounded uncertainty:

1. **Structural ignorance** — They may not know the difference between B.Sc, BCA, BBA, B.Com, B.A. or what subjects those involve.
2. **Ecosystem fragmentation** — Admission info (Samarth), school board info (JKBOSE), professional admissions (JKBOPEE), scholarships (NSP, PMSSS), government exam bodies (JKPSC, JKSSB) are all separate portals with no unified navigation layer.
3. **Self-ignorance** — Many students visiting at this stage genuinely do not know what they are interested in. They have not been exposed to enough fields to form a preference.
4. **Geography** — Access to colleges is uneven. A student in Baramulla may have different options from a student in Kathua.
5. **Trust deficit** — Students don't know which information sources are authoritative. Third-party sites spread incorrect eligibility information.
6. **Decision anxiety** — The perceived permanence of stream/course selection causes paralysis. Students fear making the "wrong" choice.

### What CareerNova Must Do
Not predict. Not rank. Not certify. Instead:

> **Help the student progressively reduce uncertainty by making the J&K education ecosystem understandable and navigable — without pretending to know their future.**

## PART C — STUDENT DECISION MAP

### C1. Class 10 Student Decisions

A Class 10 student is making ONE primary decision: **What should I study in Class 11/12?**

This breaks into:

| Decision | What They Need | Where CareerNova Helps |
|---|---|---|
| Which stream? (Science / Commerce / Arts) | Understanding what each stream involves | Explain streams without predicting career |
| What subjects are in each stream? | JKBOSE Scheme of Studies | Link to JKBOSE; explain key subjects |
| What can each stream lead toward? | Broad direction awareness | Map streams → possible UG paths (not promises) |
| What should I investigate before choosing? | Guidance on exploration | Suggest questions to ask themselves and their school |
| Are there scholarship options while studying Class 11/12? | NSP / financial awareness | Reference NSP |

**What CareerNova must NOT do for Class 10 students:**
- Recommend a specific undergraduate degree
- Predict a career outcome
- Suggest a stream as definitively "right"

### C2. Class 12 Student Decisions

A Class 12 student is making a more complex set of decisions:

| Decision | What They Need | Where CareerNova Helps |
|---|---|---|
| What fields of study could fit me? | Field exploration | Exploration model (not quiz-to-career) |
| What courses exist in J&K? | Verified course catalog | Directions + Courses data |
| Which courses am I eligible for based on my stream? | Qualification filtering | Hard eligibility filter (stream-based) |
| What is the difference between courses? | Course comparison | Compare feature |
| Where are courses available in J&K? | Verified GDC list | Colleges feature |
| What can I study after this UG degree? | Higher study pathways | Outcomes (PG, professional) |
| What entrance exams may matter? | Exam awareness | Outcomes (exam type) |
| What financial support may exist? | Scholarship awareness | Resources → NSP, PMSSS |
| Where do I actually apply? | Official portal | Link to J&K Samarth, JKBOPEE |

**What CareerNova must NOT do for Class 12 students:**
- Tell them "you are suited for X"
- Invent salary/placement data
- Rank colleges without authoritative methodology
- Submit applications on their behalf

## PART D — THE CLASS 10 vs CLASS 12 DISTINCTION IN LOGIC

This is a hard product requirement. The system must branch at the very first step.

```
Student enters CareerNova
        │
        ▼
"What class are you currently in?"
    ┌───┴───┐
Class 10     Class 12
    │              │
    ▼              ▼
Stream         Field
Exploration    Exploration
    │              │
    ▼              ▼
JKBOSE         Courses +
Resources      Eligibility
    │              │
    ▼              ▼
"What to       "Where to
investigate    study and
before         what can
choosing?"     it lead?"
```

**Class 10 flow must not show undergraduate course recommendations.**
**Class 12 flow must not ask "which stream are you planning to choose?"**

The current system treats both identically (only filtering by `classRequired`). This is insufficient.

## PART E — THE "I DON'T KNOW" STATE

This is a first-class state, not an edge case.

A student who says "I don't know my interests" should receive:

1. **Activity-based exploration questions** (not Myers-Briggs personality questions):
   - "Which of these activities sounds least boring to you?"
   - "Do you prefer working with numbers, words, living things, computers, or people?"
   - "Which school subjects do you enjoy most right now?"
   - "Are there any subjects you definitely do NOT want to study more?"
   - "Is there anything you are curious about but haven't studied yet?"

2. **An honest result**: "Based on your responses, these fields may be worth exploring — but since you're still discovering your interests, treat these as starting points, not answers."

3. **The ability to change**: Preferences set today should not lock the student into a recommendation permanently.

**What the system must NOT do:**
- Force a choice from a small list and call it a "preference"
- Interpret a vague answer as a strong signal
- Eliminate fields because the student was uncertain

## PART F — EXPLORATION SIGNAL TAXONOMY

Replace the current binary interest tags with a richer signal model:

| Signal Type | Example | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hard constraint** | Stream = Science (PCB) | Deterministic | Cannot be overridden by preference |
| **Declared interest** | "I enjoy Biology" | Moderate | Student-stated; may change |
| **Activity preference** | "I prefer lab work over writing essays" | Low-moderate | Behavioral signal |
| **Subject enjoyment** | "Chemistry is my best subject" | Low-moderate | Academic signal |
| **Aversion signal** | "I definitely don't want to do commerce" | Negative filter | Soft exclusion only |
| **Uncertainty** | "I don't know" | Neutral | Do not suppress directions |
| **Curiosity signal** | "I'm curious about computers but never tried" | Low | Worth surfacing, not predicting |

**Aversion signals should produce soft exclusion, not hard elimination** — unless there is a genuine qualification constraint.
