## 1. THE CORE VALUE PROPOSITION

> **CAREERNOVA IS NOT:**
> - Another generic India career quiz portal.
> - Another static college directory.
> - Another unverified government link bookmark page.
> - A competitor to official J&K government portals.

> **CAREERNOVA IS:**
> **The Decision Layer positioned directly above J&K's official education infrastructure.**

---

## 2. WHY CAREERNOVA EXISTS

J&K Government portals (J&K Samarth, JK DSD ITI, JKBOPEE, JKPSC, JKSSB, PMSSS) contain extensive factual data, but that data is fragmented across separate websites. A student who asks *"What should I do next?"* must open 10 different government websites and manually piece together eligibility rules, college locations, financial aid, and career outcomes.

CareerNova solves the **connection and decision problem**:
1. **Aggregates** verified J&K government datasets into a unified universal pathway graph.
2. **Hard-Filters** eligibility based on student stream, subjects, and academic stage (Class 10 vs. Class 12).
3. **Connects** disparate pathway families (Degrees + Standalone ITI Skill Trades + Backward Govt Exam Roadmaps).
4. **Enables** side-by-side comparison in a persistent Decision Workspace (`/dashboard/compare`).
5. **Directs** the student to the exact official government portal for execution (`/dashboard/nextstep`).
