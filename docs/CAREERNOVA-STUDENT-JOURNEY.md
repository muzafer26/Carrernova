**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** August 17, 2026  

---

## 1. THE 11-CHAPTER STUDENT JOURNEY

Rather than presenting a dense management admin panel, CareerNova guides the student through progressive disclosure:

```
CHAPTER 1: ABOUT YOU          (Stage, Stream, District)
CHAPTER 2: DISCOVER YOURSELF  (Interest & Signal Exploration)
CHAPTER 3: WHAT INTERESTS YOU (Disciplinary Filter Selection)
CHAPTER 4: POSSIBLE PATHWAYS  (Degrees, Skills, Govt Cadres)
CHAPTER 5: WHY THESE?         (Why Engine Rationale & Disclaimers)
CHAPTER 6: EXPLORE A PATH     (In-depth Curriculum & Entry Rules)
CHAPTER 7: WHERE CAN YOU DO IT (GDC / ITI District Local Finder)
CHAPTER 8: WHAT CAN IT LEAD TO (Post-Graduation & State Exams)
CHAPTER 9: COMPARE            (Side-by-side Heterogeneous Matrix)
CHAPTER 10: MY SHORTLIST      (Persistent LocalStorage Decision Set)
CHAPTER 11: MY NEXT DECISION  (Verified Portal Handoff & Checklist)
```

---

## 2. CHAPTER INTERACTION DESIGN

- **One Task at a Time:** Every stage presents one primary question before advancing.
- **Human Guidance:** Integrated sticker & character prompts provide calm, educational tone.
- **No Management Wall:** Full dashboard analytics only unlock after student context is established.
