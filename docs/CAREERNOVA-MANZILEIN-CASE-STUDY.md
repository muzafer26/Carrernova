# CAREERNOVA — MANZILEIN CASE STUDY
## SIH25094 | Critical Analysis of Historical J&K Career Portal

**Document Version:** 2.0  
**Date:** August 17, 2026  
**Role:** Senior Product Architect & Domain Researcher  

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Manzilein** (the J&K Career Portal, `manzilein.in`) was established by the J&K School Education Department in partnership with UNICEF and iDreamCareer as a career information portal. This case study evaluates Manzilein's design, identifies its current legacy status, and documents how CareerNova delivers the **NEXT DECISION LAYER** without duplicating past government work.

---

## 2. MANZILEIN vs CAREERNOVA CASE STUDY MATRIX

| Evaluation Dimension | Manzilein Portal (Historical Initiative) | CareerNova Decision Layer (SIH25094) |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem Addressed** | Lack of centralized career & college information in J&K schools. | High student decision uncertainty during Class 10 & 12 transition points. |
| **Core Delivery** | Static encyclopedia of generic career cards and entrance exam dates. | Interactive **Decision Layer** providing stream prerequisite filtering, shortlist persistence, and side-by-side comparison. |
| **Academic Framework** | Legacy pre-NEP 3-year degree structure. | 100% Aligned with NEP-2020 4-Year FYUGP under J&K Samarth. |
| **Pathway Diversity** | Degree-heavy catalog; vocational options secondary. | Equal first-class status for 4-Year Degrees, 6–24 Month ITI Trades, Polytechnics, and Backward Govt Exam Cadres. |
| **Decision Support ("Why?")** | Reads like a textbook; student must infer why an option suits them. | Integrated **Why Engine** explaining evidence-backed rationale ("Why this stream/course/skill is shown") and explicit non-guarantees. |
| **Current Status** | **AVAILABLE BUT LEGACY / UNCERTAIN.** Infrequently updated; out of sync with 2026–27 J&K Samarth portal. | **ACTIVE VERIFIED SYSTEM.** 100% Sourced against active 2026–27 government portals. |

---

## 3. ARCHITECTURAL CONCLUSION

CareerNova must **NOT** be "Manzilein 2.0". Manzilein attempted to solve the *information availability* problem. Today, information exists across government portals, but students face an *information fragmentation and decision paralysis* problem. CareerNova solves this by serving as the orchestration layer that connects, compares, and explains.
