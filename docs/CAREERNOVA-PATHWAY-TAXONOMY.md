## 1. PATHWAY FAMILIES

1. **Undergraduate Degree (NEP FYUGP):** 3-Year Degree / 4-Year Honours with Research.
2. **Integrated UG-PG Programme:** 5-Year Dual Degree (M.Sc/M.A./MBA).
3. **Professional Degree:** Statutory 4/5-Year Degree (B.Tech, B.Sc Nursing, B.Sc Agriculture, B.A. LL.B).
4. **Polytechnic Diploma:** 3-Year Technical Engineering Diploma (J&K BOTE).
5. **ITI Trade Certificate:** 6 to 24-Month Vocational Trade (DSD J&K).
6. **NSQF Skill Certificate:** 3 to 6-Month Skill Competency Module (NSQF Level 4/5).
7. **Apprenticeship Programme:** On-the-job industrial apprenticeship (NAPS/NATS J&K).
8. **Government Career Cadre:** Backward educational roadmap targeting JKPSC / JKSSB examinations.
9. **Professional Certification:** Autonomous professional body route (ICAI Direct Entry, ICMAI, NIELIT).
