# CAREERNOVA — JAMMU & KASHMIR STUDENT ECOSYSTEM
**SIH25094 | End-to-End Decision Architecture**

---

## 1. Ecosystem Overview
CareerNova operates as the **student-facing decision layer** over Jammu & Kashmir's official educational landscape. Instead of duplicating government databases or redirecting students to a confusing list of links, CareerNova converts official data into a **progressive, step-by-step guided story experience**.

```
[ OFFICIAL J&K SYSTEMS ]
Directorate of Colleges | J&K Samarth | JKSDM / ITI | JKBOPEE | JKPSC | PMSSS
                               │
                               ▼
[ CAREERNOVA DECISION LAYER ]
Guided Story Journey (One Question Per Screen)
  ├── 1. About You (Class 10 vs 12, Stream, District)
  ├── 2. Goal Exploration & Discovery
  ├── 3. Pathway Families (Academic, ITI, Govt, Polytechnic)
  ├── 4. Progressive Detail (What will I study? Can I get in?)
  ├── 5. Local GDC Institutions (Where can I study in J&K?)
  ├── 6. Verified Outcomes & Future Opportunities
  └── 7. Official Handoff (J&K Samarth / JKBOPEE Portal)
```

---

## 2. Decision Support Pathways

### A. Class 10 Students
- **Core Decision:** Which stream to choose (Science PCM, Science PCB, Commerce, Arts) or whether to enter an ITI vocational trade (COPA, Electrician).
- **Government Authority:** JKBOSE (Syllabus & Stream Eligibility) / J&K DSD (ITI Admissions).
- **CareerNova Role:** Explains stream trade-offs, subjects, and future degree paths without enforcing premature specialization.

### B. Class 12 Students
- **Core Decision:** Selecting undergraduate programs (B.Sc, B.Com, B.A., BCA, BBA) and identifying local Government Degree Colleges (GDCs).
- **Government Authority:** Directorate of Colleges J&K / J&K Samarth Admission Portal (`jk.samarth.ac.in`).
- **CareerNova Role:** Directs student to eligible GDCs in their district, explains NEP 2020 4-year options, and guides application submission.

### C. Skill & Practical Seekers
- **Core Decision:** Short-term employment skills and ITI trade certificates.
- **Government Authority:** Directorate of Skill Development (J&K DSD) / JKSDM.
- **CareerNova Role:** Provides skill-gap context and direct admission links.

### D. Government Service Seekers
- **Core Decision:** Preparing for civil services (JKPSC KAS) or non-gazetted posts (JKSSB).
- **Government Authority:** JKPSC / JKSSB.
- **CareerNova Role:** Maps required undergraduate degrees to government examination eligibility.
