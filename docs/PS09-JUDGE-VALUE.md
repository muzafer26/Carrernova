## 1. Direct Answers to Core Evaluation Questions

### Q1: Why CareerNova if government websites already exist?
* **Answer:** Official government websites (like J&K Samarth, JKBOSE, or JKBOPEE) are **transactional portals**. They process applications, conduct exams, and issue seat allotments once a student already knows what to apply for. CareerNova is the **pre-application decision and exploration layer**. It guides confused students to figure out *what* they qualify for and *where* they should apply *before* locking preferences on Samarth.

### Q2: Why not just use Google search?
* **Answer:** Searching Google for "what to do after 12th PCM in J&K" returns thousands of SEO-optimized commercial coaching blogs, unverified aggregator articles, and out-of-date PDF notices. Google does not enforce statutory eligibility filtering or connect student inputs directly to verified J&K Government Degree Colleges. CareerNova provides a curated, evidence-backed decision pathway anchored exclusively to official sources.

### Q3: Why not just use ChatGPT or a generic AI chatbot?
* **Answer:** LLMs frequently hallucinate course eligibility, invent non-existent college cutoffs, manufacture synthetic placement percentages, or confuse J&K statutory rules with generic national boards. CareerNova operates on a **strictly deterministic, source-backed data model** where eligibility strings and resource links are 100% verified against primary university statutes.

### Q4: Why not use the Samarth portal directly?
* **Answer:** Samarth requires students to manually browse through hundreds of raw college-programme combinations without explaining how those programs relate to the student's 10+2 stream eligibility, personal activity preferences, or post-graduation study options. CareerNova organizes these choices into intuitive, qualified "Fields Worth Exploring".

## 3. Measurable Student & Institutional Value

1. **Reduced Application Misalignment:** Prevents students from submitting invalid program preferences on Samarth that get rejected due to stream eligibility mismatches.
2. **Rural & Remote Access:** Provides students in hilly and remote J&K districts with transparent access to official scholarship schemes (PMSSS, NSP) and institutional degree options.
3. **Eradication of Misinformation:** Replaces commercial aptitude tests that promise "career destiny" with honest, non-deterministic educational guidance.
