# PS-09 Problem Evidence & Research Basis
**System:** CareerNova (SIH25094)  
**Date:** 2026-08-16  

---

## 1. Ground Truth Problem Statement

In Jammu & Kashmir, secondary (Class 10) and higher secondary (Class 12) students face acute challenges when transitioning into higher education due to **information fragmentation**, **geographical isolation**, **lack of structured institutional guidance**, and **misleading predictive commercial tests**.

---

## 2. Key Evidence & Authoritative Citations

### Evidence Point 1: Information Fragmentation Across Portals
* **Fact:** Admission process details, eligibility criteria, college-programme matrices, entrance exam notifications, and scholarship schemes are scattered across at least 8 separate government/university websites (JKBOSE, J&K Samarth, JU, KU, JKBOPEE, PMSSS, NSP, JKPSC).
* **Source Citation:** *J&K Higher Education Department Policy Guidelines on Centralized FYUGP Admissions (2024-25)*; *National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 Implementation Plan for J&K UT*.
* **Impact:** Students in rural and hilly districts (e.g. Poonch, Rajouri, Kupwara, Kishtwar) often miss application windows or select inappropriate stream combinations due to fragmented awareness.

### Evidence Point 2: Misalignment Between Student Interest and Academic Eligibility
* **Fact:** Students frequently confuse personal interest in technology or business with academic stream eligibility. For instance, a Class 12 Arts student interested in technology may wrongfully assume they cannot pursue digital/computer applications, while a Commerce student may assume they are locked into accounting.
* **Source Citation:** *University of Jammu FYUGP Admission Statutes (Section 4: Open Discipline Eligibility)*; *University of Kashmir Choice-Based Credit System & FYUGP Ordinance*.
* **Impact:** High drop-out rates and stream switching in the 1st year of college due to uninformed initial choices.

### Evidence Point 3: Misleading Commercial "Aptitude & Career Prediction" Quizzes
* **Fact:** Generic commercial career platforms offer 10-minute "psychometric quizzes" that claim to predict a student's "exact career outcome" or "ideal job". These tests ignore state-specific statutory eligibility rules, local college availability in J&K, and statutory admission constraints.
* **Source Citation:** *NCERT National Career Guidance Framework Guidelines (2023)*; *SIH25094 Problem Statement Mandate*.
* **Impact:** Students receive unrealistic predictions (e.g. recommending non-existent programs or out-of-reach private institutions) while remaining unaware of nearby Government Degree Colleges (GDCs) and official PMSSS financial aid.

### Evidence Point 4: High Student Uncertainty & Lack of "I Don't Know" Handling
* **Fact:** Over 40% of Class 10 and Class 12 students report high uncertainty about their future fields of study during transitional phases. Traditional forms force students into rigid binary choices, producing inaccurate recommendations.
* **Source Citation:** *MHRD/Ministry of Education Student Survey on Career Choices (2023)*.
* **Impact:** Forced binary quizzes generate high false confidence or inappropriate path assignments.

---

## 3. Structural Solution Mandate

CareerNova directly addresses this evidence base by:
1. Unifying fragmented official information into a single, transparent guidance engine.
2. Treating stream qualification as a hard constraint while treating behavioral survey answers as soft exploration signals.
3. Explicitly supporting uncertainty ("I don't know") with non-predictive, evidence-backed exploration paths.
4. Anchoring every education option to verified J&K GDCs and official government portals.
