# CAREERNOVA — GOVERNMENT SYSTEM GAP ANALYSIS
**SIH25094 | Problem & Decision Layer Value Proposition**

---

## 1. What Existing Government Portals Provide
Government portals in Jammu & Kashmir excel at **official administration, application processing, merit list publication, and legal record management**:
- **J&K Samarth Portal:** Seat allocation, application submission, fee payment.
- **JKBOPEE Portal:** Entrance exam registration, merit ranking, document verification.
- **JKSDM Dashboard:** Aggregate state statistics, training partner registrations.
- **JKPSC / JKSSB Sites:** Recruitment notifications, examination admit cards, results.

---

## 2. What Is Missing from Government Systems (Student Decision Gaps)
While government systems perform transaction processing, they leave major decision gaps for students:

1. **Overwhelming Data Dumps:** Raw college lists and notifications leave students confused about which program suits their academic background.
2. **Lack of Comparative Context:** A student cannot easily compare a B.Sc in Computer Science vs. a BCA vs. an ITI COPA certificate on official portals.
3. **Siloed Systems:** High education, skill development, professional entrance exams, and scholarships operate in separate subdomains without a unified student decision flow.
4. **No Progressive Disclosure:** Forms require immediate input without explaining *why* options are available.

---

## 3. How CareerNova Solves These Gaps
CareerNova fills these exact gaps without duplicating government infrastructure:
- **Unified Guided Journey:** Combines degree, skill, professional, and government service pathways into one step-by-step experience.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Reveals information one question at a time (Class → Stream → District → Goal → Pathways → Detail → Local Colleges → Handoff).
- **Source-Backed Evidence:** Explains *why* options appear based on verified J&K Directorate data.
- **Seamless Official Handoff:** Guides the student directly to official application portals (`jk.samarth.ac.in`, `jkbopee.gov.in`, `jkdsd.in`) when they are ready to act.
