## 1. STATE INITIATIVE INTEGRATION MATRIX

| Initiative Name | Governing Body | Student Stage | Purpose | CareerNova Non-Duplication Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **J&K Samarth Higher Ed Portal** | Directorate of Colleges J&K | Class 12 Graduates | Centralized UG college & FYUGP admissions across GDCs | **FILTER / SEARCH & DECISION LAYER:** Hard-filters eligibility & maps colleges before directing students to Samarth for application. |
| **J&K DSD ITI & Skill System** | Dept of Skill Development J&K | Class 10 & 12 | ITI admissions, polytechnics & NSQF skill modules | **EXPLANATION & STANDALONE DISCOVERY:** Surfaces 6–24 month trade options directly as first-class routes without hiding them behind degree cards. |
| **JKBOPEE Entrance System** | J&K BOPEE | Class 12 Science | Professional entrance (B.Sc Nursing, MBBS, B.Tech) | **REQUIREMENT MAPPER:** Details statutory eligibility, CET entrance structure, and clinical internship progression. |
| **JKPSC & JKSSB Exam Systems** | JKPSC & JKSSB | Class 12 / Graduates | Recruitment for Gazetted (JKAS) & Non-Gazetted cadres | **BACKWARD EDUCATIONAL MAPPER:** Maps backward educational stepping stones required from Class 10/12 to qualify for state cadre exams. |
| **AICTE PMSSS J&K Scheme** | AICTE / Higher Ed | Class 12 J&K Domiciles | Financial aid (₹1L maintenance + tuition waiver) | **CONTEXTUAL MATCHING ENGINE:** Matches PMSSS allowance eligibility inside Decision Workspace based on stream & domicile. |
| **Aao Baat Karein Helpline** | Directorate of School Ed | Class 9–12 | Tele-counselling & mental health support | **CONTEXTUAL LINK:** Directs students seeking emotional support to official helplines rather than offering fake AI therapy. |
