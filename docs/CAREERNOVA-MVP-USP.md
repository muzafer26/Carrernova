## 1. UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITION (USP)

> **CAREERNOVA USP:**  
> **"CareerNova connects the fragmented Jammu & Kashmir education ecosystem directly to the student's actual decision."**

CareerNova is **NOT** another generic career quiz, a static college directory, or a duplicate government portal. It is the **Decision Layer** positioned directly above official state systems (J&K Samarth, JK DSD ITI, JKBOPEE, JKPSC, JKSSB, PMSSS J&K).
