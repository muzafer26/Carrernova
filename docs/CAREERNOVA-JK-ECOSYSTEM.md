## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & DECISION LAYER PHILOSOPHY

CareerNova operates as the **DECISION LAYER** positioned directly above the existing Jammu & Kashmir education ecosystem. Rather than duplicating existing government portals (such as J&K Samarth, JK DSD, JKBOPEE, or JKPSC), CareerNova aggregates, contextualizes, and connects fragmented government datasets to resolve a student's core question:

> *"Given my current stage, stream, and district in J&K, what realistic pathways exist, how do they compare, what financial support applies, why is each option shown, and what official portal should I use next?"*

## 2. GOVERNING AUTHORITIES & ECOSYSTEM INTEGRATION

| Authority / Initiative | Purpose & Domain | Official Web Portal | CareerNova Decision Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Directorate of Colleges J&K** | Higher Education Dept, ~142+ GDCs | `https://jkadmissions.samarth.ac.in` | Directs students to verified GDC degree programmes & NEP-2020 FYUGP major choices. |
| **University of Jammu (JU)** | Affiliation & Statutes (Jammu Division) | `https://jammuuniversity.ac.in` | Validates academic statutes for UG/PG degrees and law (B.A. LL.B). |
| **University of Kashmir (KU)** | Affiliation & Statutes (Kashmir Division) | `https://www.kashmiruniversity.net` | Validates academic statutes for UG/PG degrees in Kashmir Division. |
| **Cluster Universities (CLUJ / CUS)** | Constituent Colleges (Jammu & Srinagar) | `https://clujammu.ac.in` / `https://cusrinagar.edu.in` | Surfaces 5-year integrated UG-PG degree programs. |
| **SKUAST Jammu & Kashmir** | Agricultural, Veterinary, Forestry | `https://skuast.org` / `https://skuastkashmir.ac.in` | Maps 4-year B.Sc Agriculture & Veterinary pathways via SKUAST-CET. |
| **JKBOSE** | School Education & Board Examinations | `https://jkbose.jk.gov.in` | Guides Class 10 students through JKBOSE streams and subject choices. |
| **Directorate of Technical Ed / DSD J&K** | Skill Development, 54+ ITIs, Polytechnics | `https://jkdsd.in` | Surfaces 6-24 month standalone ITI trades & polytechnic engineering diplomas. |
| **JKBOPEE** | Professional Entrance Examinations | `https://www.jkbopee.gov.in` | Maps entrance requirements for B.Sc Nursing, Paramedical, and MBBS. |
| **JKPSC & JKSSB** | Public Service Commission & Selection Board | `https://jkpsc.nic.in` / `https://jkssb.nic.in` | Backward-maps educational stepping stones for state gazetted and non-gazetted jobs. |
| **AICTE PMSSS J&K & NSP** | Financial Aid & Scholarship Schemes | `https://aicte-jk-scholarship-gov.in` | Dynamically matches PMSSS maintenance/tuition allowances and NSP post-matric schemes. |
